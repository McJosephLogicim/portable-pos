# Payment method directory and icons

The bank presets use the PDIC insured bank directories dated 15 April 2026 for [universal and commercial banks](https://www-origin.pdic.gov.ph/files/BSDStats/Bank%20Directory_KB%20_website.pdf), [thrift banks](https://www-origin.pdic.gov.ph/files/BSDStats/Bank%20Directory_TB%20_website.pdf), [rural and cooperative banks](https://www-origin.pdic.gov.ph/files/BSDStats/Bank%20Directory_RB%20_website.pdf), and [digital banks](https://www-origin.pdic.gov.ph/files/BSDStats/Bank%20Directory_DB%20_website.pdf). MariBank is listed under digital banks based on [BSP Circular Letter 2026-035](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/2026/CL-2026-035.pdf).

Wallet and issuer names use the [Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas e-money issuer directory](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Lists/Directories/Attachments/7/emi.pdf) and [Philippine Payments Management Inc. member directory](https://www.philpayments.org.ph/member-institutions). The catalog also recognizes common consumer brand names. It is a bundled snapshot for offline use.

Cash uses original artwork. The following brand marks belong to their respective owners.
